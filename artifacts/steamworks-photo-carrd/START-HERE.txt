STEAMWORKS PHOTO — Carrd kit
============================

What's in this folder
- images/     Photos, favicon, and share image
- COPY.txt    Every line of website text, ready to paste
- LINKS.txt   Every URL
- STYLE.txt   Colors, fonts, and how the page is stacked

How to rebuild this on Carrd
1. New Carrd site. Theme: dark. One page.
2. Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headings) + Outfit (body).
3. Page background: #0b0a09
4. Follow STYLE.txt top to bottom. Paste from COPY.txt.
5. Upload images from images/. Swap in your own photographs
   wherever a placeholder is used — especially the gallery.

The hero, robe, window, silk, tulle, edge, and steam images
are lighting studies, not client work. Replace them with yours.
